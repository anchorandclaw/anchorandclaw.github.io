These services are designed to work together as a practical and proactive security program for small businesses and organizations. Each engagement focuses on reducing attack exposure, improving resilience, and helping you stay ahead of problems before they become incidents. Organizations can choose individual services based on their needs or bundle all six together for a more comprehensive review of their overall security posture.

Account Security Assessments & Identity Protection

A proactive review of accounts, authentication practices, access controls, password hygiene, recovery options, open source intelligence exposure, and common email risk areas. Weak passwords, reused credentials, missing multi-factor authentication, and poorly organized account access are still some of the most common causes of compromise today. I help organizations improve identity security through stronger authentication controls, safer account management practices, password managers, suspicious login alerting, and practical recommendations designed to reduce the chances of preventable account takeover incidents before they happen. The goal is to make it significantly harder for attackers to gain access in the first place while helping your organization build safer long-term security habits.

Starting at $200 per engagement
☁

Secure Platform Configuration & Attack Surface Reduction

Secure environment setup and hardening for cloud platforms, business infrastructure, and Windows, macOS, or Linux endpoints. I implement preventative controls designed to reduce unnecessary attack exposure, limit opportunities for lateral movement, and strengthen the overall defensive posture of the organization. Modern adversaries frequently rely on built-in operating system functionality to avoid detection instead of deploying obvious malware. Small configuration weaknesses and overlooked defaults are often enough to create serious risk. This service focuses on reducing those opportunities through practical hardening, safer administrative practices, endpoint controls, logging improvements, and security-focused operational guidance built around how real attacks actually occur.

Starting at $200 per engagement
🔁

Resilience & Disaster Recovery Planning

I review and implement thoughtful backup and disaster recovery strategies for both on-premise and cloud environments, validate that systems are functioning as intended, and document recovery procedures so organizations are confident responding during a real incident. This includes backup validation, recovery testing, recovery walkthroughs, and simulated ransomware-style recovery exercises designed to help teams understand exactly how to restore critical systems and data under pressure. The unfortunate reality of ransomware, account compromise, accidental deletion, and system failure is that one of those situations eventually becomes inevitable. The goal is simple: make sure a bad day does not become a business-ending one.

Starting at $200 per engagement
🚨

Incident Response & Recovery Support

When something already looks wrong, access is compromised, or systems start behaving unexpectedly, I help contain the problem, secure the environment, and walk organizations through the recovery process step by step. This includes practical containment guidance, rebuilding affected systems, recovery support, investigation assistance, and helping organizations understand how the incident occurred in the first place. Security incidents are stressful, especially when teams are unsure what happened or whether their systems are actually safe again. My goal is not just to stop the bleeding and clean up the issue, but to help organizations regain confidence in their systems, accounts, and overall security posture without adding more panic or confusion to the situation.

Starting at $200 per engagement
🧭

Threat Modeling & Risk Management

Sometimes the hardest part of cybersecurity is simply knowing what actually matters. This service focuses on understanding your environment from an adversarial perspective without performing direct technical testing against systems. I work with organizations to build asset inventories, develop site maps, identify critical systems, evaluate trust relationships, and model realistic attack paths against the assets that matter most. The goal is not to overwhelm organizations with technical jargon or expensive enterprise frameworks. It is to help businesses understand where meaningful risk exists, prioritize improvements thoughtfully, and make practical security decisions based on realistic threat exposure.

Starting at $200 per engagement
🔍

Vulnerability Management & Security Testing

Most successful attacks do not begin with sophisticated exploits. They begin with exposed services, weak authentication, risky configurations, forgotten systems, or small security gaps that were never reviewed closely. This service focuses on actively validating and testing those weaknesses through attack surface review, vulnerability validation, configuration analysis, and targeted security testing designed around realistic attack paths. Unlike the hardening and configuration services, the goal here is not to implement the fixes directly. The goal is to identify exposure, validate realistic attack opportunities, demonstrate how those weaknesses could be abused by an adversary, and provide practical recommendations that help organizations strengthen their defenses before those gaps become incidents.

Starting at $200 per engagement
